Good questions

FAQ

Is WinCut really free?

Yes — completely. No watermark, no export limits, no ads, no trial. The Microsoft Store listing is free and so is the app.

Does it upload my videos anywhere?

Never. WinCut processes everything on your device using bundled open-source FFmpeg components. It makes no network requests at all.

What is "smart cut" exactly?

Normal lossless cutting snaps to keyframes, so your cut point can drift. Smart cut re-encodes only the small edge at the cut so the video starts on your exact frame — while the rest of the file is copied bit-identically. Frame-exact and quality-neutral.

Which formats are supported?

Inputs: MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI, WMV, TS, MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A, AAC, OGG, Opus and more. Outputs: MP4 (H.264/H.265/AV1), WebM (VP9), GIF, and audio files (MP3, M4A, FLAC, WAV, Opus).

Can I join multiple videos into one?

Yes — the Join feature combines files in the order you choose. Files with matching parameters are joined losslessly in seconds; mismatched files are automatically normalized first. WinCut tells you exactly what will happen before you start.

Will it run on my PC?

Windows 10 (1809+) or Windows 11, x64 or ARM64 — the Store handles installation and updates automatically.
